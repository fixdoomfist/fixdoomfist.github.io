<h2>Punch momentum override on stairs</h2>
<p>While some abilities like Brig’s Bash or Vendetta’s Overhead Slash can affect Doomfist’s momentum in almost any circumstance, the effect is quite negligible. But, when Doomfist is using Rocket Punch to go up stairs and slopes, the abilities start to tangibly affect the player’s momentum, to the point that a Brigitte’s Bash can completely override Rocket Punch’s movement long enough to change or hinder its trajectory.

The list:
Anran's Inferno Rush, Ashe's Coach Gun, Bastion's A-36 Tactical Grenade, Brigitte's Whip Shot, Domina's Sonic Repulsors, Hazard's Jagged Wall, Illari's Outburst, Jetpack Cat's Purr, Lúcio's Soundwave, Mauga's Overrun, Orisa's Javelin Spin, Pharah's Concussive Blast, Reinhardt's Shield Slam, Roadhog's ult, Shion's Joyride, Vendetta's Whirlwind Dash, Venture's ult, Venture's melee, Winston's ult, Wuyang's Guardian Wave.</p>

<h2>Domina stun lasts through punch</h2>
<p>Bug found by slofs!

When Domina uses her Sonic Repulsors on Doomfist, it’s fair to assume that using any movement ability would override the effect, but, for some reason, Doomfist’s Rocket Punch does not. You can get stunned by just hitting a wall using Rocket Punch if it happens soon after you get hit with Sonic Repulsors, and the direction of the punch does not matter.</p>
</div>

<div class='bug_card'>
<h2>Punch cooldown not resetting</h2>
<p>Thanks to `itztonii` for providing a fresh clip with the replay code.

This bug remained a mystery for quite some time, but turns out it’s extremely easy to replicate. When you get empowered punch (i.e. the charge bar overfills on the damage) by an ability that stuns you (i.e. Sigma rock or Orisa javelin) and hold the Rocket Punch button at the same time, Rocket Punch goes on cooldown instead of it being reset.</p>
</div>
