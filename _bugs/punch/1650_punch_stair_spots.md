I wasted 5 hours of my life to find ~50 more spots where Rocket Punch seems to collide with a wall when it shouldn’t. For simplicity sake, since all these spots have something to do with stairs, they are referred to as “stair spots”.

<!-- SPLIT -->

Exact spots for replicating are shown in the linked Youtube videos.

1. As Doomfist, punch the enemy towards the bugged stair spot.
2. Observe how they get stunned on nothing as if they have hit a wall.
3. Some of these spots may require specific characters or specific distances between the character and the stairs to be stunned.
