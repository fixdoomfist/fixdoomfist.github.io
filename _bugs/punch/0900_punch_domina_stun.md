Bug found by slofs!

When Domina uses her Sonic Repulsors on Doomfist, it’s fair to assume that using any movement ability would override the effect, but, for some reason, Doomfist’s Rocket Punch does not. You can get stunned by just hitting a wall using Rocket Punch if it happens soon after you get hit with Sonic Repulsors, and the direction of the punch does not matter.
