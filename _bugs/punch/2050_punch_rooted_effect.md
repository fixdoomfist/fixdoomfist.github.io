When a target is in a state where they are unable to move, like Junkrat’s Trap, or self-inflicted ones, like Junkrat’s RIP Tire or D.Va remeching, you cannot land a wall hit on them. These instances conflict with other “rooted effect” examples, as people can freely get wall hit if they are close to a wall, such as Zarya’s Graviton Surge and Sigma’s Gravitic Flux.

Note: The D.Va interaction is not on hydra.

<!-- SPLIT -->

Junkrat Steel Trap example:

1. As a Junkrat on the same team as Doomfist, place a trap flush against a wall.
2. As an enemy, step into the placed trap.
3. As Doomfist use Rocket Punch on the trapped enemy.
4. Observe how, even though the enemy is touching the wall, they do not receive wall hit damage.
