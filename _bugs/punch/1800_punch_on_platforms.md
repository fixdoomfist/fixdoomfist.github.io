When Doomfist tries to punch on rising platforms, such as Mei's Ice Wall and Lifeweaver's Petal Platform, Rocket Punch does not work most of the time. It either hits seemingly nothing and stops, or goes through the target.

<!-- SPLIT -->

This bug is quite difficult to reproduce and may require many attempts.

Mei Ice Wall example:

1. As Mei, place Ice Wall beneath both yourself and the enemy Doomfist.
2. As Doomfist, try to hit Mei with Rocket Punch as soon as you begin to be lifted by Ice Wall.
3. Observe how Doomfist either stops in place or phases through Mei without colliding.
