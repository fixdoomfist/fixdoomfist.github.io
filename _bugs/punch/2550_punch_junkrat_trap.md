When using Empowered Punch towards a Junkrat trap, it often just destroys the trap as soon as Doomfist gets stuck on it.

<!-- SPLIT -->

1. As Junkrat, place a Steel Trap.
2. As Doomfist, use Empowered Rocket Punch directly towards the placed Steel Trap.
3. Observe how the Steel Trap catches the Doomfist and immediately breaks.
