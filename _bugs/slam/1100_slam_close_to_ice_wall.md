Doomfist’s Seismic Slam is consistently not registering damage on targets that are within “gaps” of Mei’s Ice Wall. This can be easily abused.

<!-- SPLIT -->

1. As Mei, place an Ice Wall, and go forward to it until you end up in one of the "gaps" of the wall.
2. As Doomfist, cast Seismic Slam towards Mei.
3. Observe how Mei receives no damage.
