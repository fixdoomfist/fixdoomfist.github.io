If a target is hit by Rocket Punch it will receive knockback. But, if during the time the target is also hit by Seismic Slam, the enemy will stop dead in their tracks.

<!-- SPLIT -->

The easiest way to reproduce this bug is with two friendly Doomfists. Though it is possible to reproduce this bug with a single Doomfist, as shown in the linked Youtube video.

1. As the first Doomfist, punch an enemy with Rocket Punch.
2. As the second Doomfist, use Seismic Slam so that it hits the punched enemy instantly after it has been hit with Rocket Punch.
3. Observe how the enemy receives no knockback.
