Airslam is an extremely old bug where slam lands midair, which nullifies the ability. I have gathered all consistent ways to get airslam that I am currently aware of: I tried my best to explain them in the linked video.

<!-- SPLIT -->

Edge airslam example:

1. Have an enemy stand on a platform above Doomfist.
2. As Doomfist, while standing below the platform, slam upwards.
3. Doomfist should try to land on the very edge of the platform.
4. As he lands, press S to go backwards while slamming towards the enemy.
5. Observe as the enemy receives no damage from Seismic Slam.
