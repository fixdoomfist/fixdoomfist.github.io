Doomfist cannot hit anyone with his Seismic Slam ability on floors that change elevation rapidly, unless he lands directly in front of the target.

<!-- SPLIT -->

1. As Mei, place an Ice Wall beneath yourself so that you stand towards an edge of the placed wall.
2. As Doomfist, cast Seismic Slam towards Mei so that you land on the Ice Wall as it just starts to rise.
3. Observe how Mei receives no damage.
