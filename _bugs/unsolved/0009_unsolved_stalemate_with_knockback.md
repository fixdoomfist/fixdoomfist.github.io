Another extremely rare bug. In a stalemate interaction, both sides of the stalemate should have their respective abilities cancelled when knocked down. However, clips seem to show Doomfist's rocket punch applying knockback after the player himself is already stunned by the stalemate. Due to the "bug" listed in the intended section of the website, this often leads to the person being hit with Rocket Punch completely negating the stun effect.

Unsolved for 6+ months, cannot replicate for the life of me. May be connected to elevation changes and/or difference in momentum.

I've also become aware of a certain Japanese creator who appears to have discovered and replicated the bug, and their video is also linked. However, I have not been able to replicate their findings, and since the video is quite old, it's probably outdated.
