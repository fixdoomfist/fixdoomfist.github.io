Vendetta's Projected Edge can deal damage to Doomfist head-on through his block if it is thrown at a specific angle away from him.

<!-- SPLIT -->

1. As Doomfist, start blocking while facing directly towards Vendetta.
2. As Vendetta, start casting Projected Edge directly towards the Doomfist.
3. Observe as the damage is blocked.
4. Keep casting the ability while slowly moving your camera to the right until the very edge of the ability is hitting Doomfist.
5. Observe how, at some point, the ability starts dealing full damage without being blocked.
