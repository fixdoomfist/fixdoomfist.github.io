Seismic Slam seems to be able to override the momentum of Orisa’s spear and Sigma’s rock.

<!-- SPLIT -->

This bug is quite difficult to reproduce and may require many attempts.

Sigma Accretion example:

1. As Sigma, cast Accretion towards Doomfist.
2. As Doomfist, right as you get hit by Accretion, cast Seismic Slam.
3. Observe how Doomfist keeps the momentum of Seismic Slam while being stunned.
