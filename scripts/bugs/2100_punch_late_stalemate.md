Doomfist is able to cancel his punch ability by pressing space while keeping the momentum. This “tech” is one of Doomfist’s most core mechanics, used by players of all skill levels. This allows for better map traversal, getting more distance out of an ability, and much more.

However, for a few frames after the ability has been cancelled, it is possible to trigger a stalemate interaction. This results in him entering the stunned state while still retaining the momentum of the punch cancel.

<!-- SPLIT -->

1. As two Doomfist on opposing teams, punch directly into each other.
2. As one of the Doomfists, cancel Rocket Punch with the jump button a split second before collision.
3. Observe how, after cancelling punch, Doomfist still gets stunned as if he still was in Rocket Punch, but now with full momentum of punch cancel.
