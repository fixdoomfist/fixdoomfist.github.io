If you hold crouch while using Seismic Slam and press ultimate at the right time, once you enter your ultimate, your camera teleports and starts looking in a specific direction.

<!-- SPLIT -->

This bug is timing-based and may require many attempts.

1. As Doomfist, cast Seismic Slam.
2. As soon as you land, in the middle of the landing animation, use Meteor Strike.
3. Observe how Doomfist's camera snaps to a specific direction.
