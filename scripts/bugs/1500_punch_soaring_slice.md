For some unintuitive reason, when Doomfist punches Vendetta that is using her Soar ability at the right time, Vendetta takes full knockback, recovers, and, after recovery, begins to pull herself towards the previously thrown blade. This punishes players trying to deny the character an easy escape and has no intuitive counterplay.

<!-- SPLIT -->

This bug is quite difficult to reproduce and may require many attempts.

1. As Vendetta, cast Soaring Slice in the air.
2. Wait for the sword to stop in mid-air, right before Vendetta starts moving towards it.
3. As Doomfist, punch Vendetta with Rocket Punch at that exact moment.
4. Observe how Vendetta moves towards her thrown sword after the stun animation ends.
