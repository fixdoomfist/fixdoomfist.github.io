Another extremely rare bug. In a stalemate interaction, both Doomfist and the other player get stunned like normal, but the other player also gets applied knockback. This can lead to the player hitting a wall, which, as written in the intended section, overrides the stun effect.

Unsolved for 6+ months, cannot replicate for the life of me. Seems to be connected to elevation changes and/or difference in momentum.
