Bug found by `salte.`!

In the ending animation of Dancing Blaze Anran has immunity to knockback created by Doomfist Rocket Punch.

<!-- SPLIT -->

1. As Anran, cast Dancing Blaze.
2. As Doomfist, hit Anran with a charged Rocket Punch as soon as she exist the Dancing Blaze animation.
3. Observe how Anran receives no knockback from the ability.
